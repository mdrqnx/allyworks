Delivery Process

From analysis to operations,
one seamless delivery model

One team owns the entire build and carries it straight through to operations and maintenance.

  1. STEP 01

    Analysis & design

    We analyze the business environment and requirements, then design the target system's architecture and scope.

  2. STEP 02

    Development & build

    We develop the applications and set up the infrastructure according to the design.

  3. STEP 03

    Testing & transition

    After verifying functionality and performance, we move the system smoothly into production and train users.

  4. STEP 04

    Operations & maintenance

    Performance, incident, backup, and version management keep the system stable and available.
